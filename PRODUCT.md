# Browser Recipes

People use Browser Recipes to repeat tasks in signed-in browser apps while they work in another tab. They pick an installed recipe, check its results, and use workflows for tasks that span apps. Contributors inspect pages and test drafts in developer tools. Scripts and agents can call the same recipes and workflows.

Design for a narrow browser panel and full extension pages. Put runnable recipes first. Explain what a run needs in terms of the action the user takes, such as choosing an account or approving a write. Keep browser diagnostics available to the person fixing a failure. Use cool blue, crisp dividers, and compact recipe rows.
